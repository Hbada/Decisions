This project is my first Ren'py visual novel, about characters I designed in 2018. One has diabetes and the other is the sidekick. The goal is many adventures in their teens (and maybe other time periods too), at times sharing a diabetic (type 1) perspective.
Basically, the characters and their adventures are autobiographical stories that collect my memories of things I missed out on as a T1D, and things I might've experienced that a non-diabetic would miss out on, during the most influential early-teen years of old-fashioned type 1 diabetes (the kind you get in childhood, although nowadays it arrives at any age apparently).
